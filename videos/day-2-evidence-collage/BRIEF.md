## Intent

A ~56-second, mobile-first fondof promo for an X thread and the Kiro hackathon.
**The viewer is the protagonist.** The story opens on THEIR pain (generic AI skills that hallucinate and don't fit), introduces three real sources by name (Paul Graham, Peter Thiel, kepano), shows fondof forging them into a fitted skill for a real repo, demonstrates the integrity gate (rejecting a bad draft), and closes by handing the tool to the viewer.

The collage-animation reference is visual inspiration only; this piece proves Kiro + GPT-5.6 Terra + HyperFrames can fit that thinking to fondof rather than recreating a Remotion project.
